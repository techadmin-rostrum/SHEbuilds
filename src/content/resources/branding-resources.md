Create a simple, strong look for your business that people will remember and trust.

Branding means how your business looks, sounds, and feels to others. It includes your name, logo, colours and message. A good brand helps customers remember you, trust you, and come back.

Your brand shows:

- Who you are
- What you do
- What makes your work special (handmade, local, honest, women-led)

## Business Name Toolkit

Your business name is the first thing people see. It should be simple, easy to remember, and show what you do.

### Naming tips

- Easy to spell and say
- Shows what you do or what makes you unique
- Short and memorable
- Feels personal or local

### Brainstorming prompts

1. What do you sell? → *bangles, pickles, embroidery*
2. Where do you live or what is your community known for? → *Rajgrah village, mangoes, hand embroidery*
3. What special words in your language mean beauty, power or trust? → *Shakti, Sundar*
4. Do you want to include your name or your daughter’s name? → *Rekha’s Creations, Asha Threads*

## Making a Logo

A logo is a small picture or design that represents your business. You can draw it by hand or make it on free apps.

### Three easy logo options

1. **Text Logo** — Just your business name in nice letters → *SHAKTI THREADS*
2. **Symbol + Text** — Add a small icon → *a flower + “Sundari Soaps”*
3. **Drawing logo** — A hand-drawn picture of what you sell → *saree, pickle jar, scissors*

### Free tools

- [canva.com](https://www.canva.com) — Choose a “Logo” template
- [logoai.com](https://www.logoai.com) — Type your business name for options

### Logo ideas

- A flower, leaf, or item related to your product
- Your name in beautiful writing
- A colour background with a simple symbol

## Create a Mood Board

A mood board helps you decide your business style — colours, fonts, images and feelings.

### Steps

1. **Colour:** Choose 3 colours that match your product  
   - Main colour: ______________  
   - Second colour: _____________  
   - Extra colour: ____________  
   *Example: Main — Red · Second — Gold · Extra — Cream*
2. **Font:** Try big or small letters, bold, elegant, or handwriting styles.
3. **Images:** Collect photos or patterns — fabric swatches, pictures of your work, designs from market stalls.
4. **Feelings:** Words like “clean”, “warm”, “handmade”.

Use your notebook to draw or paste mood board ideas!

## Sharing Your Brand Story

People love the story behind your business. It helps customers trust you and feel proud to support you.

### Prompts (write short, simple answers)

- Why did you start this business? → *I wanted to earn my own money and help my children.*
- What do you care about the most? → *Quality, tradition, hygiene, handmade skills.*
- What makes your work special? → *Made at home, family recipes, made with love.*

Write 3 lines to tell your story — then share it with customers when you introduce yourself.
