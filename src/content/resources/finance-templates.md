Understand your money. Plan it simply. Grow your business with confidence.

This section gives you simple tools and examples to help you manage your costs, savings, and profits. Don’t worry if this feels new — you don’t need to be good at maths to use these tools.

Just follow the steps with a notebook and use the ready-made tables below.

## Startup Costs Template

**What are startup costs?**  
Startup costs are the money you need to start your business — tools, supplies, rent, or licenses. It is usually a one-time cost to get everything ready.

**Why are they important?**  
They help you know how much money you need to start. If you know the cost, you can save up, borrow wisely, or ask for help — so you are not surprised later.

### How to use this table

| Column | What to Write | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item I Need to Buy | Name of the thing you need | sewing machine, cloth, cooking pot, mirror |
| How Many? | Number of that item | 1 sewing machine, 5 sets of cloth |
| Cost per Item (₹) | Price of 1 item | If 1 cloth set is ₹100, write ₹100 |
| Total for that item | How many × price | 5 × ₹100 = ₹500 |

At the bottom: add up the totals. That final number is how much money you need to begin.

## Monthly Expenses Template

**What are monthly expenses?**  
Regular costs you pay every month to keep your business running — raw materials, transport, electricity, data, or loan payments.

**Why are they important?**  
They show how much you must earn each month. If sales are low, you can cut some costs. It also helps you avoid debt or loss.

### Value of your labour

Your labour is the time, effort and energy you put into running your business. Even if no one pays you directly, your work has real value.

**How to calculate it:**

1. Decide how many hours you work in a month.  
   *Example:* 5 days a week × 2 hours = 40 hours a month
2. Choose a fair hourly rate (often ₹30–₹50 per hour).
3. Multiply: 40 × ₹30 = ₹1,200 per month
4. Add this to your monthly expenses table under “Your Own Labour”.

| Column | What to Write | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expense Type | What you spend on each month | ingredients, bus fare, mobile recharge |
| Amount per Month (₹) | How much in one month | Estimate from last month if needed |
| Your Own Labour | Hours × fair rate | 20 hours × ₹30 = ₹600 |

Add up Amount and Labour columns for your true monthly cost.

## Revenue Forecast Template

**What is a revenue forecast?**  
A smart guess for how much money your business will make — weekly or monthly — based on what you sell.

**Why it matters:** It shows if your idea can make enough money, whether you are growing, and helps when you apply for a loan.

| Column | What to Write | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What I Sell | Product or service name | Haircut, bangles, food plate |
| Price (₹) | Charge per unit | 1 haircut = ₹50 |
| How Many in a Month? | Expected sales | 5 customers/week ≈ 20/month |
| Total Earned (₹) | Price × number | ₹50 × 20 = ₹1,000 |

Add all row totals for expected monthly income.

## Savings Tracker Template

**What is a savings tracker?**  
A simple way to record each time you save — date and amount — so you can see progress toward a goal.

**Why it matters:** It helps you buy bigger tools later, grow the business, and build a habit of planning money — even with small amounts.

| Column | What to Write | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Date | When you saved | 1 June or “Week 1” |
| Amount Saved (₹) | How much | Even ₹20 or ₹50 is good |
| Purpose | Why you are saving | mirror, tools, rent later |
| Total So Far (₹) | Running total | Shows growth over time |

Each time you save more: add a new row and update Total So Far.
